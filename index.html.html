Standard EEE Formula & Constants Handbook
Series RLC Natural Frequency & Damping
ω0 = 1 / √(LC)  |  ζ = (R / 2)√(C / L)

Quality factor Q = ω0L / R; Half-power bandwidth BW = f0 / Q.

Maximum Power Transfer Theorem
Pmax = Vth2 / (4·Rth)  at  RL = Rth

Transfer efficiency at maximum power condition is η = 50%.

CRO Lissajous Phase Angle
Δφ = sin−1(Yintercept / Ymax)

Frequency ratio fY / fX equals horizontal tangencies divided by vertical tangencies.

Transformer Efficiency & Regulation
η = (x·S·cosφ) / (x·S·cosφ + Pi + x2Pcu)

Maximum efficiency occurs at load fraction x = √(Pi / Pcu,FL).

DC Machine Back EMF & Torque
Eb = (P·Φ·Z·N) / (60·A)  |  τ = 0.159·Φ·Z·Ia·(P/A)

Speed N ∝ (Va − IaRa) / Φ. Never open shunt field winding while running.

3-Phase Induction Motor Slip & Torque
s = (Ns − Nr) / Ns  |  Ns = 120f / P

Maximum pull-out torque occurs at slip sm = R2' / √(Rth2 + (Xth + X2')2).

Controlled Single-Phase SCR Rectifier
Vdc,full = (2Vm / π)·cos(α)  (Continuous CCM)

For resistive load R: Vdc,full = (Vm / π)(1 + cos α).

DC-DC Buck & Boost Converters
Buck: Vo = D·Vin  |  Boost: Vo = Vin / (1 − D)

Inductor ripple ΔiL = (VL,on·D·Ts) / L.
