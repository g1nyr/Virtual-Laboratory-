Standard Laboratory Safety & Reference Constants
  1. Oscilloscope Earth Reference: Both channels of a standard CRO share a common chassis ground. Never connect channel ground clips across ungrounded high-side components.
  2. DC Machine Field Protection: Never open the field winding of a running DC shunt motor (loss of flux causes runaway overspeed). Never start a DC series motor unloaded.
  3. Inductive Converter Switching: Interrupting current in an inductive load generates high L·(di/dt) voltage spikes; always verify freewheeling diode paths before firing SCRs or MOSFETs.
  4. Standard Reference Constants: Thermal voltage VT = 25.85 mV (at 300 K); Synchronous speed Ns = 120f / P; Base frequency f = 50 Hz.
